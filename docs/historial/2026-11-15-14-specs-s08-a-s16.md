# Specs S08 to S16 implemented, except S11

| [S03](../specs/S03-ci-y-despliegue.md) | in progress | `feat/s03-cierre` | Timing tests out of `npm test`, with `npm run test:perf`. Deployment is still missing |

The branches are stacked, each on top of the previous one, in that order: `feat/s14-robustez` contains everything. Those still "in progress" have the code done and lack manual checks, which each spec lists.

## What was measured

| Measure | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Automated tests | 201, all passing on the second laptop |
| Browser tests | 13 flows, all passing, in about 8 minutes with software WebGL |
| Initial JavaScript | 271 KB compressed, of 350 allowed |

## What came up along the way

- **A 5° margin lowers the link at the summit of Malapert from 100% to 51%** on the 30-day mission: Earth spends a good part of the month very low over the relief ([S09](../specs/S09-ventanas-de-contacto.md)).
- **The eclipse of 3 March 2026 covers the Sun for 134 minutes at Apollo 11 and 69 at Malapert**, within 4 minutes of the maximum given by `astronomy-engine` ([S08](../specs/S08-energia-y-bateria.md)).
- **The crest of Connecting Ridge is 2.3 km from permanent shadow** and has sunlight for 100% of the November mission: power and science at once ([S16](../specs/S16-sombra-permanente.md)).
- **A design regression:** the power and communications blocks stretched the map to 1871 px high. It was fixed in [S12](../specs/S12-modo-educativo.md): the status column scrolls inside itself.
- **Forcing the graphics card left the test browser without WebGL2** on the second laptop; the browser tests use software WebGL ([S15](../specs/S15-pruebas-de-navegador.md)).
- **The printed report took five pages**: hiding the app with `visibility` left its gap. Now it is mounted separately and fits on one ([S10](../specs/S10-exportacion.md)).

## What is left

- Integrating Andrés's S11, which is already in `main`.
- The manual checks of each spec in progress and the deployment of [S03](../specs/S03-ci-y-despliegue.md).
- The north pole of [S13](../specs/S13-catalogo-y-polo-norte.md) and the static copy of [S03](../specs/S03-ci-y-despliegue.md), both of which can be cut.

## Lo que salió por el camino

- **Un margen de 5° baja el enlace de la cima de Malapert del 100% al 51%** en la misión de 30 días: la Tierra pasa buena parte del mes muy baja sobre el relieve ([S09](../specs/S09-ventanas-de-contacto.md)).
- **El eclipse del 3 de marzo de 2026 tapa el Sol 134 minutos en Apollo 11 y 69 en Malapert**, a 4 minutos del máximo que da `astronomy-engine` ([S08](../specs/S08-energia-y-bateria.md)).
- **La cresta de Connecting Ridge está a 2.3 km de la sombra permanente** y tiene luz el 100% de la misión de noviembre: energía y ciencia a la vez ([S16](../specs/S16-sombra-permanente.md)).
- **Una regresión de diseño:** los bloques de energía y comunicaciones estiraban el mapa hasta 1871 px de alto. Se arregló en [S12](../specs/S12-modo-educativo.md): la columna de estado se desplaza por dentro.
- **Forzar la tarjeta gráfica dejaba sin WebGL2** al navegador de pruebas en el segundo portátil; las pruebas de navegador usan WebGL por software ([S15](../specs/S15-pruebas-de-navegador.md)).
- **El informe impreso ocupaba cinco páginas**: ocultar la app con `visibility` dejaba su hueco. Ahora se monta aparte y cabe en una ([S10](../specs/S10-exportacion.md)).
