# Specs S06 and S07 implemented

## What was done

| Spec | Status | What it brings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [S06](../specs/S06-terreno-multirresolucion.md) | done | Horizon over LOLA grids of 20, 40 and 80 m; precomputed horizons for small servers |
| [S07](../specs/S07-validacion-y-coordenadas.md) | done | [VALIDACION.md](../VALIDACION.md), source of each coordinate and pitch recomputed |

The changes were left in the working directory, with no commits: from this date on Kevin makes the commits by hand.

## What was measured

| Measure | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Automated tests | 123 with fine grids; 118 and 5 skipped with `LUMINA_HIRES=0`. All pass |
| Horizon on 20 m terrain | 55 ms (it was 30 ms at 240 m) |
| Point search, fine grid, 20 m terrain | 5.2 s with 8 threads (8.8 s with 4) |
| Precomputed horizons | 36 files, 424 KB in total |
| 20-year solar visibility at Connecting Ridge C2, at 2 m | 82.1%, against 82.0% published |
| Longest shadow at that point | 165 h, the same as published |
| Sites within the range of NASA's maps at ±60 m | 10 of 11, for the Sun and for Earth |

## What came up along the way

- [Two catalogue sites fell off the ridge on fine terrain](2026-11-14-09-trescientos-metros.md): their coordinates had 300 m of leeway.
- **NASA publishes the maps, not just the figures.** The spec planned to compare with values quoted from two papers. The LOLA team's 18.6-year maps are public, so the comparison was made at every catalogue site.
- **At ground level the numbers do not match; at 2 m they do.** The differences at 0 m reach 20 points and are explained in the document, with nothing tuned.
- **Connecting Ridge is no longer an Artemis III candidate region.** It was on the 2022 list and not on that of October 2024. The label was corrected.
- **The search got slower.** On fine terrain it went over 10 s. It was solved with more worker threads and with step tables shared between rays; the result of each horizon is identical to the one before optimising.
- A timing test of [S01](../specs/S01-escenarios.md) failed once while other tasks kept the processor busy, and passed on the three following runs.

## What is left of this block

- **S03:** still to be deployed. Something new to bear in mind: with the fine grids the server uses about 400 MB; without them it serves the catalogue with the precomputed horizons.
- **Unconfirmed coordinates:** the VIPER point and the Chang'e 7 point stay as representative.
- **Browser tests:** still outside the repository, as in the [previous block](2026-11-14-08-specs-s01-a-s05.md).

## Lo que salió por el camino

- [Dos sitios del catálogo caían fuera de la cresta con terreno fino](2026-11-14-09-trescientos-metros.md): sus coordenadas tenían 300 m de margen.
- **NASA publica los mapas, no solo las cifras.** La spec preveía comparar con valores citados de dos artículos. Los mapas de 18.6 años del equipo LOLA son públicos, así que la comparación se hizo en todos los sitios del catálogo.
- **A ras de suelo los números no coinciden, a 2 m sí.** Las diferencias a 0 m llegan a 20 puntos y están explicadas en el documento, sin ajustar nada.
- **Connecting Ridge ya no es región candidata de Artemis III.** Estaba en la lista de 2022 y no en la de octubre de 2024. La etiqueta se corrigió.
- **El buscador se volvió más lento.** Con terreno fino pasaba de 10 s. Se resolvió con más hilos de trabajo y con tablas de pasos compartidas entre rayos; el resultado de cada horizonte es idéntico al de antes de optimizar.
- Una prueba de tiempos de [S01](../specs/S01-escenarios.md) falló una vez mientras otras tareas ocupaban el procesador, y pasó en las tres repeticiones siguientes.

## Qué queda de este bloque

- **S03:** sigue pendiente de desplegar. Novedad a tener en cuenta: con las mallas finas el servidor usa unos 400 MB; sin ellas sirve el catálogo con los horizontes precalculados.
- **Coordenadas sin confirmar:** el punto de VIPER y el de Chang'e 7 quedan como representativos.
- **Pruebas de navegador:** siguen fuera del repositorio, como en el [bloque anterior](2026-11-14-08-specs-s01-a-s05.md).
